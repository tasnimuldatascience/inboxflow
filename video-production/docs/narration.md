# Narration — More than a click

Voice: locally synthesized Kokoro af_heart; no voice impersonation.

## 0–8s / hook

What if your emails could do more than get clicked?

## 8–18s / reveal

Meet Inbox Flow. An interactive email platform for modern ecommerce. One workspace, from your first idea to your customer's next action.

## 18–30s / connect

Connect your commerce stack, sync your catalog, and bring your products into your email workflow. Here, Shopify and Klaviyo run in a clearly labeled local sandbox.

## 30–48s / build

Start with a template. Drag in real catalog products. Add collections and subscription controls. Fine-tune your colors and typography. Then save, preview, and export a sandbox draft, from one flexible editor.

## 48–66s / alive

A message becomes a starting point. Preview the email, then explore products and choose options in the hosted experience. Email support follows the inbox's actual capabilities. A browser fallback carries the rest.

## 66–82s / commerce

Choose a variant, adjust the quantity, and continue to sandbox checkout. Then manage the next subscription delivery, review the change, and confirm. Fewer unnecessary steps, with clear confirmation at every important moment.

## 82–92s / feedback

Collect a product review. Discover a customer's preferences. Their feedback is saved, and the profile updates in the sandbox.

## 92–106s / measure

Back in the workspace, see recorded interactions, subscription actions, and confirmed sandbox orders. Ask focused questions with a transparent, rule-based analytics assistant. Real activity. Clear context.

## 106–112s / close

Inbox Flow. Make every email a moment worth opening.
